# Xend Feature List

## Couple Level

A shared level system based on Bond Points earned from chatting, completing rituals, playing games, and doing challenges together.

**Availability:** Free

## Couple Chat

A private messaging space made only for the couple, focused on secure and personal conversations.

**Availability:** Free

## Couple Daily Rituals

Small daily prompts that help couples check in, reflect, and stay emotionally connected.

**Availability:** Free

## Silly Games

Light and playful games for couples, like questions, "who's more likely," fun dares, and quick bonding games.

**Availability:** Free + Pro expansion

## Daily Challenges

Simple challenges partners can send or complete for each other, like compliments, sweet messages, or thoughtful actions.

**Availability:** Free

## Emotional Status

Each partner can set how they feel, helping the other person understand their mood without needing to ask directly.

**Availability:** Free

## Pet Feature

A shared virtual pet that grows as the couple interacts, completes rituals, plays games, and earns Bond Points together.

**Availability:** Pro

## Take A Break

Lets couples temporarily pause notifications or interaction pressure while keeping the private space intact.

**Availability:** Future / Free or Pro

## Breakup / End Space

A respectful way to disconnect the couple space and choose what happens to shared data, memories, and access.

**Availability:** Future / Pro
